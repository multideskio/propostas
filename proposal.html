Decoupled Hybrid Architecture

A comprehensive proposal to rebuild BDS Floors for ultimate performance, absolute security, and zero maintenance. Keep your WordPress panel, transform your user experience with Next.js.

View Investment & Approve Explore Live Mockup

Why Hybrid Next.js Architecture?

Your current website relies on standard WordPress running Elementor. Over time, plugins, page-builders, and database queries have bloated the load times. By decoupling the frontend and using Next.js, we compile the site into static assets and dynamic pages using Incremental Static Regeneration (ISR) prior to visitors accessing it.

1. Admin Backend

You manage posts, floor images, and locations exactly as you do today in your WordPress panel.

2. Next.js Hybrid API

Our Next.js engine automatically queries your WordPress database and builds optimized pages on-demand or statically.

3. Global Edge CDN

Raw assets are distributed globally. The visitor loads your website in under 0.5 seconds.

100% Content & SEO Preservation
